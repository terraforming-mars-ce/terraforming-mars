First tagged version, covering development from August 2025 to
February 2026.

## Added
- Real-time multiplayer: create a game, share the link, and play
  together over WebSocket with server-side rules enforcement.
- A 3D Mars board with ocean, greenery, city and special tiles,
  placement animations and sounds.
- The project card engine, driven by a JSON database of the base game
  cards with generated artwork for all 453 cards.
- Corporations, starting card selection, the production phase,
  milestones, awards and final scoring.
- Card hand, actions, effects and played-card views, a dark space
  theme and an animated 3D background behind the menus.
- Development mode with admin commands and a tile placer.
