## New
- Cities are built from distinct buildings, including Capital's spire.
- Lakes with sandy shores, denser forests, and planets with
  atmospheres and moving sunlight.
- Drag a card onto the board to play it, with a preview of where it
  goes.
- Lobby chat and more game settings.
- Markers show which actions you can take.

## Improved
- New card frames, corporation art and resource icons.
- Menus work with the keyboard.
- Construction smoke and sounds, quieter effects, and a new
  soundtrack.

## Fixed
- Search For Life gives 3 VP when it holds science.
- You can no longer pick extra preludes past the limit.
- Dragging a card no longer moves the board.
- Phobos cities sit on the moon's surface.
