## Major update: Colonies

Colonies is now playable.

- Build colonies and trade with them for resources.

## New
- A solar system view with orbiting planets you can fly between.
- Project funding: buy seats in shared projects and earn their
  rewards when they complete.
- Asteroid impacts, an orbital station, spaceships and the Mohole tile.
- The end of the game counts VP with an animation, shows score graphs
  and lets you replay the game.
