## Major update: Venus Next

Venus is now a game option, with its own planet to terraform.

- Travel between Mars and Venus and place tiles on a 3D Venus.
- A Venus track in the sidebar.
- The Venus corporations.

## New
- Preludes: pick prelude cards after your corporation.
- Bots: the host can hand an empty seat to a bot.
- Spectate games, chat, and pick your player colour.
- Hosts can kick players and end the game.
- Experimental cards: The World Tree and Front of the Line.
- Bonuses on the global parameter tracks.
- Space and Enter hotkeys, background music, and birds on greenery.

## Fixed
- The Mars board now matches the official Tharsis map.
- Greenery from cards must go next to your own tiles.
- 13 cards were missing their negative VP.
- Event card tags no longer count toward requirements.
- Starting cards are now random.
- Arctic Algae and other ocean effects now trigger.
- Cards that lower production check that you can afford it.
- Sound starts on your first click.
