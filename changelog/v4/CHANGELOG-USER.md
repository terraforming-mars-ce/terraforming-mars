## Major update: New maps

Hosts can pick from ten Mars maps in the lobby.

- Hellas and Elysium milestones and awards, picked at random each game.
- Map tiles can give production, oxygen, temperature or oceans.

## New
- Final greenery: turn leftover plants into greenery before scoring.
- Aridor, Arklight and Stormcraft corporations.
- Stormcraft can pay for Heat to temperature with floaters.
- Buy 1 random card in the production phase.
- Browse all cards from the in-game menu.
- Lobby settings for map, expansions and player limit.
- A score projection graph at the end of the game.

## Improved
- An animated production phase with sound.
- Better-looking oceans.
- Global parameters show how far along they are.

## Fixed
- Colony trade rewards are correct again.
- Poseidon waits for you to place your colony.
- The World Tree counts only adjacent greenery.
- Destroy and steal effects can be skipped when nobody has the
  resource.
- SCORE and GRAPHS stay clickable at the end of the game.
