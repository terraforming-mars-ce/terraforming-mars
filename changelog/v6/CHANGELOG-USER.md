## Major update: Play on your phone

The game now works on phones in landscape.

- Hand, actions, projects, tableau, players and log on their own
  screens.
- Pinch to zoom, two fingers to pan, tap a hex to see it.
- Install the game as an app from the start page.
- Lighter graphics for phones and older computers.

## Major update: Bots

Bots join your games with their own personalities.

- They chat, hold grudges and write a recap after the game.
- The host can retry a bot that gets stuck.

## New
- Chat emotes, typing indicators and reactions.
- Trade fleets and more colony rules.
- Mars has terrain, and its climate follows the global parameters.
- A rebuilt Nuclear Zone with an on-screen blast.
- Corporations and preludes are shown to everyone at the start.
- Music controls with track names.

## Improved
- Payments show exactly what you pay, including steel, titanium and
  card resources.
- Card text is split into clear sections.
- Corporation first actions happen at the start of your turn.

## Fixed
- Only the game's own awards can be funded.
- Wild tags and requirement discounts count correctly.
- Popovers stay on screen and close with Escape or a tap outside.
